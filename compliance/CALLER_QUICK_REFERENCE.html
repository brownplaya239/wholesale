Opening — every call, word for word

"Hi, is this {first name}? This is {your name} calling from HouseSoldNJ — we're local real estate investors, and our calls are recorded. I'm calling about {property street} — is that still yours?"
  • "Are you agents / realtors?" Tell the truth: "Yes — Sumeet Sancheti, who owns HouseSoldNJ, is a licensed agent. We also buy directly, and he'll walk you through every option." Never deny it.
  • Never drop "our calls are recorded." Some owners pick up in states where recording without telling them is illegal.
  • Not the owner? "Who would I speak with about the property?" "He passed away"? Slow down, condolences, ask who handles the estate. That's a lead.
  • Live voicemail only, in your own voice: "Hi, this is {name} calling from HouseSoldNJ about {property street}. You can reach us at {number}. Thank you."

On the call

  • Confirm the owner first.
  • Motivation before price: "What's got you thinking about it?" · "What would you do if it sold?"
  • Capture: condition, timeline, occupancy, decision makers, what they hope to get ("roughly where would it need to be for you?").
  • Fill the fields during the call. Disposition last. Notes within 5 minutes.
  • Close with a next step: book Sumeet, or a dated callback.

Never

  • Say a price, offer, range, commission or listing terms. "Sumeet will go over real numbers with you himself."
  • Keep talking after "stop calling" in any words. Apologize, confirm, disposition DNC.
  • Keep pitching a property listed with an agent. Thank them, end the call, Already Listed.
  • Bring up back taxes, liens or how the list was built. If the owner raises it, listen and ask what they'd like to do.
  • Dial outside the queues, from a personal phone, or a number typed by hand.
  • Drop a recorded voicemail. Claim to be anyone you're not.

Hot vs Warm

Hot = all of: confirmed owner or decision-maker · real intent to sell · a reason (motivation) · an actionable timeline · property details captured · open to an offer.

Warm = confirmed owner willing to discuss; urgency, price or timing unresolved.

Neither = "might sell someday", only asks what we'd pay, or no real selling indication.

Fields for Warm/Hot: owner confirmed · motivation · condition · timeline · asking price · occupancy · decision makers · next action · next follow-up.

When and where

  • Mon–Sat, 9:00 am–8:00 pm in the owner's time zone. No Sundays, no federal holidays.
  • Queue order: Q1 Inbound → Q2 Power 9a-8p. Q3 only after each contact's window opens. Q4 Manual (FL/OK/MD) single line only. Q5 Land as its own session.
  • Max 2 dials per owner per day (WAVV double dial on a no-answer). Never a third, and never redial after someone answered, a voicemail, a wrong number or "stop calling."
  • Every call is recorded and reviewed. Missed opt-outs and price talk are flagged.
