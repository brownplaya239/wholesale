# 701-703 Monroe Ave, Asbury Park — Entitlement Dossier & Deal Screen

**Block 2608, Lot 7 (Monmouth 1304) · pulled 2026-08-13 · status: NOT a deal at ask**

## Bottom line

The broker is pricing an R-1-surrounded parcel off the approval across the
street. $2,499,999 is almost exactly the land residual of the *perfect*
outcome — Council rezones the site via redevelopment-plan amendment, a 30-yr
PILOT is granted, and parking relief matches what 700 Monroe got — priced at
100% certainty and zero carry. Risk-adjusted today the dirt screens at
**~$1.0-1.1M**; as-is floor is **~$560K** (Monmouth annual reassessment).
Ask / risk-adjusted ≈ **2.3x**. The site is genuinely in the path of growth;
the *price* assumes the growth has already arrived.

## 5. Massing & parking test (see `massing_test.py`)

Envelope assumed = what a plan amendment would plausibly grant (the CS-zone
pattern): ground parking podium + 4 residential floors in a ~45-ft plane,
~82% coverage, small corner retail. All three programs fit the same
envelope — the difference is unit size, not building size:

| Units | Mix (st/1BR/2BR) | Res GSF | Avg NSF/unit | Podium stalls | RSIS req | Local practice (1.5/u) | Plan std 0.8/u (EV-adj) | Shortfall vs plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 35 | 4/19/12 | 36,080 | 825 | ~20 | 65 | 52 | 28 (25) | 5 |
| 40 | 8/22/10 | 36,080 | 722 | ~20 | 74 | 60 | 32 (29) | 9 |
| 42 | 10/22/10 | 36,080 | 687 | ~20 | 78 | 63 | 34 (30) | 10 |

- **Parking is the binding constraint, not massing.** One podium level
  yields ~20-26 stalls on this lot. Even the redevelopment-plan standard
  (~0.8/unit, the 700 Monroe number) leaves a 5-10 stall shortfall → the
  project needs the same **design exception** 700 Monroe is asking for
  (26 provided vs 40 required). RSIS/garden rates (65-78) are unreachable —
  viable only via the plan's own standard + transit justification.
- 35 units is the "comfortable" program (825 NSF avg, shortfall ~5); 42
  units is 700-Monroe-equivalent density with sub-700 NSF studios/1BRs.
- Under current R-1: **one single-family house (+ ADU)**. A 35-42 unit ZBA
  use/density variance on an R-1 lot of this size is not a realistic path;
  the only credible route is the Council plan-amendment mechanism above.

## 6. Deal screen (calibrated so 700 Monroe-type deals pencil)

Rents $3.55/SF/mo blended (west-of-Main new build), 20% affordable at ~55%
of market, 5.35% cap, hard $250/SF res + $115/SF podium, 20% soft:

| Scenario (42u) | Land residual |
|---|---|
| Entitled + 30-yr PILOT + parking relief (best case) | **~$2.45M** |
| Entitled, conventional taxes | ~$0.6M |
| Risk-adjusted today (40% entitle odds, 2 yrs, 15%/yr) | **~$1.08M** |
| As-is floor (assessed, annual reassessment) | ~$0.56M |

Sensitivity: ±$0.15/SF/mo rent ≈ ±$0.85M on the entitled residual — the
best case only exists with a PILOT (every west-side comp carries one:
900-904 Springwood took a 30-yr PILOT to deliver 92 units).

**Verdict: $2.5M = the seller keeping 100% of the developer's entitlement
and PILOT upside.** A rational buyer pays that only after Council has
adopted the plan amendment — which is the seller's work left undone.
